## Purpose and Scope

This project addresses **Brief C (Money)** within *Build 1 — Data to Insight in Kalpa Health*. The group will develop an auditable financial reconciliation claim, reproducible evidence, documented analytical decisions, and an action-oriented presentation.

## Deliverables and Acceptance Criteria

| ID | Deliverable | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **The Claim** | Slide 3 presents a single-sentence claim stating the exact denominator (**79,246 Q2 invoices**) and period (**Q2 2026**). |
| **D2** | **Reproducible Evidence** | A cold run of **Notebooks 01–06** in a fresh environment generates identical numbers in **under 10 minutes**. |
| **D3** | **The Three Logs** | `approach-log.md`, `decisions-log.md`, and `challenges-log.md` are fully completed, with no empty cells. |
| **D4** | **The Action** | Slide 6 explicitly separates actions into **Chase (Rs 48.41 L)**, **Wait (Rs 62.67 L)**, and **Fix (Rs 72.69 L)**. |
| **D5** | **Write-ups and Video** | Four individual reflective write-ups (**600–900 words each**) and a video demo link are submitted. |
